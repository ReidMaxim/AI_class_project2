Duck.ai said

The issue was a VPN path MTU problem.

MTU is the largest packet that can travel through an interface without being fragmented. Your HTB VPN path was likely unable to properly carry larger TCP packets because of VPN encapsulation or blocked Path MTU Discovery. This is sometimes called a PMTU black hole.

That explains the behavior:

Small packets, such as TCP handshakes and HTTP headers, arrived normally. 

The server began sending the larger HTML response.
Those packets were dropped somewhere along the VPN path.
curl received 0 out of 19386 bytes and waited until timing out.
Lowering tun0 to 1200 forced your system to use smaller packets that successfully fit through the VPN path.
